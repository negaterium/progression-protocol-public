# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participation in this project should be respectful, constructive and focused on
the work. Treat people and their contributions with patience, assume good faith,
and explain disagreements with evidence and clear technical reasoning.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, deliberate disruption,
and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report a concern privately through the process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or
contact the repository maintainer through the private channel listed in the
repository profile. Do not include credentials, private database exports or
personal player data in a report. Reports will be reviewed and handled as
confidentially as the available tooling allows.
